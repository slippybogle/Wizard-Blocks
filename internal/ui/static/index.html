Creature log

Every job (one block template) spawns a creature whose rarity is the job's best share as a % of network difficulty: Common <50%, Uncommon 50–63.3%, Rare 63.3–76.7%, Epic 76.7–90%, Legendary (dragon) 90–<100%, Block ≥100%. Luck = share of equally-sized jobs whose best share would have been lower.
